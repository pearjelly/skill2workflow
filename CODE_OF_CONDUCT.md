# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in `skill2workflow` respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or role.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes:

- giving specific, evidence-based, and constructive feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and operating constraints;
- accepting responsibility, correcting mistakes, and preserving privacy;
- focusing disagreement on technical and product decisions, not people;
- helping maintain a safe environment for users and contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information,
or sustained disruption of project collaboration.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.
Actions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and
avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

For immediate safety concerns, use GitHub's abuse reporting tools or applicable
local emergency channels. When project maintainer action is needed, open a
detail-free moderation request using the repository's dedicated issue form and
ask for private contact. Do not publish names, allegations, screenshots, or
other sensitive details in that public request.

Good-faith reports will be handled as privately as the available project tools
allow. Retaliation against a reporter or participant is unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces, as well as when someone officially represents the project.
